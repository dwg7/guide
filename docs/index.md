# DWG7 Guide

DWG7 Access Layer is a practical guide for understanding how DWG7 works, finding a relevant entry point, and trying a small, visible step forward. It is designed for people who are curious but do not need a formal institutional or technical briefing to begin.

## What this guide is for

This guide helps outsiders make sense of a distributed, research-oriented, and experiment-led environment. Rather than acting as a membership campaign or a governance document, it helps readers connect the work to their own context and decide where to start.

## The five products

The pages form one journey:

Understand → Find a path → Try something → Learn the culture → Return and observe
{: .journey}

- **[DWG7 in 5 Minutes](dwg7-in-5-minutes.md)** A concise orientation for people meeting DWG7 for the first time.
- **[Onboarding Paths](onboarding-paths.md)** Entry routes based on role, interest, and practice.
- **[Good First Experiments](good-first-experiments.md)** Low-barrier experiments for observing and testing.
- **[DWG7 Field Guide](field-guide.md)** A practical FAQ on the norms, culture, and responsibilities of the space.
- **[Monthly Snapshot](monthly-snapshot.md)** A reusable way to translate activity into a readable narrative.
{: .cards}

## Ready to try something?

Start with one small experiment from [Good First Experiments](good-first-experiments.md). Pick something that fits your time, tools, and curiosity, and record what you observed rather than trying to solve a whole problem at once.

## Note

This guide does not govern participation, certify members, or act as an official technical approval process. It is intended to support understanding, connection, and experimentation. It does not replace the public record of the group or its repositories.
{: .note}
